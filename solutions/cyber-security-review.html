How long does a cybersecurity review take?−

The time depends on the size of your network and the scope of the review. Most cybersecurity reviews are completed within a few days to a few weeks.

What is included in a cyber risk assessment?+

The assessment reviews firewall configurations, network segmentation, authentication controls, endpoint security, privileged access, vulnerabilities, and security governance.

How frequently should a cybersecurity review be carried out?+

Most organisations schedule annual reviews or reassess security after major infrastructure, operational, or regulatory changes.
