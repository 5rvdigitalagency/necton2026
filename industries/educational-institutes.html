How do you manage our school firewalls and web filters? −

Network Consultancy configures and maintains your existing security hardware. We handle all routine updates and blocklist adjustments to keep your network compliant with UK safeguarding legislation.

What happens if our Wi-Fi fails during a lesson or exam? +

Our engineers monitor your switches and access points remotely. If a connection drops, we troubleshoot the hardware immediately to restore service without needing an on-site IT technician.

Can you work within our specific budget and funding cycles? +

Yes. We map your hardware upgrades to your actual capital funding dates. This ensures your finance team receives predictable procurement costs throughout the year.
