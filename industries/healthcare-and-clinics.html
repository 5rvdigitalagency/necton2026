What network security measures do financial institutions use? −

Financial organisations protect their networks with several layers of security. These include firewalls, data encryption, multi-factor authentication, intrusion detection systems, continuous monitoring and strict access controls.

What are the most common cyber threats financial organisations face? +

Financial organisations are commonly targeted by phishing, ransomware, malware, distributed denial-of-service (DDoS) attacks and insider threats. These attacks may steal sensitive data, disrupt services or gain unauthorised access to financial systems.

Why is continuous network monitoring important? +

Continuous monitoring helps identify unusual activity before it becomes a serious security incident. It enables faster response to potential threats, supports compliance requirements, reduces the risk of disruption and helps keep financial services available to customers.
