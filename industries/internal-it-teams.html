What exactly is network consultancy, and how does it assist the internal IT team? −

Network consultants provide specialised networking knowledge that the internal IT team's schedule does not permit them to develop within the organisation.

The consultant will design, troubleshoot, and secure networks in conjunction with the internal IT team. The consultant applies their expertise to the networks that require more focus than the internal IT team can provide in their daily activities.

When should an organisation use network consultancy services? +

Organisations typically bring in Network Consultancy during a network upgrade, an expansion to new sites, or a security issue that requires expertise that the internal team doesn't have time to build in-house.

What are the benefits of working with a network consultancy? +

Network Consulting takes over the day-to-day monitoring and troubleshooting of the network, eliminating the need to compete for resources with other operational priorities. This allows early detection and resolution of problems that could disrupt your network's operation. Like other organisations that engage external service providers, the internal IT department has more time to focus on its overall business objectives, rather than continuously maintaining the network.
